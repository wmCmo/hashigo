花 と 鼻

Same sound, until the particle.

On their own, 花 and 鼻 both go low then high. After 花 the pitch drops; after 鼻 it stays up. A clip of the bare word cannot carry that, so the card does.

Or hear it, then name it

Identify mode plays the word and hides it. You find the kanji from the sound. When the 型 shows you had it wrong, one switch moves the card to the other mode.

A reveal is a walk

Each press uncovers one blank, then the next side. forgot is there from the first step; remembered waits until you have seen it all.

Shadow the clip

Loop it, slow it to 0.75×, record yourself and play the two side by side. No 0.5×: stretching that far smears the fall you are listening for.

FSRS-6, two buttons

Remembered or forgot. Intervals grow to a year, aimed at 90% recall. Ten new cards a day, and a word waits until you have met its kanji.

On your device

The whole database lives in the browser and works offline. A laptop and a phone catch each other up whenever they next connect.

Cards that link

Link one card to another, see what links back, and keep the sentence where you first found a word. Ctrl+K finds anything.

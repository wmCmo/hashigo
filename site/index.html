はしご · a ladder

Learn the word, and how it rises and falls.

Hashigo is a flashcard app for Japanese built around pitch accent. Every word carries its accent pattern, every example sentence is marked mora by mora, and the audio waits until you have said the word yourself.

花 と 鼻

Two words that sound the same until a particle follows.

Said on their own, 花 and 鼻 are both low then high. The difference appears on the next syllable: after 花 the pitch drops, after 鼻 it stays up. A clip of the bare word cannot carry that, so Hashigo puts the pattern on the card.

花flower尾高

はな＼が. The fall lands on the particle, so 尾高 and 平板 only part ways in a sentence.

鼻nose平板

はなが. No fall at all. On the review card, a 平板 phrase carries no mark.

In this corpus, 2,148 of the 3,830 labelled words are 平板 or 尾高. For all of them, the label is the only place the difference shows.

練習

Built for one way of studying.

Say it, then hear it

A card in produce mode shows the kanji and stays silent. You say the reading and the contour aloud, reveal, and compare. Audio before the reveal would hand you the answer.

Or hear it, then name it

A card in identify mode plays the word and hides it. You find the kanji from the sound. When the 型 label shows you had an accent wrong, one switch moves the card to produce mode.

A reveal is a walk

Each press uncovers one blank, then the next side. forgot is there from the first step. remembered waits until everything is on screen.

Shadow the clip

Replay, loop, 0.75× and 1.25×, and record yourself to play back beside the clip. No 0.5×: stretching the audio that far smears the very fall you are trying to hear.

FSRS-6, two buttons

Remembered or forgot. Intervals up to a year, aimed at 90% recall. Ten new cards a day, and a word waits until you have met its kanji.

Yours, on your device

The database runs in the browser and stays there. Reviews work offline, and a phone and a laptop keep each other up to date when they next connect.

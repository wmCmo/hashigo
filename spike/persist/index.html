Will the browser keep Hashigo's data?

Throwaway test page. It asks for storage persistence, opens the real 7,716-card database in OPFS, and can fill ~700 MB to see what the quota does.

What this device says

1 · persistence and the database

After a reload the database should still be there. That is the test for "does it survive closing the app".

2 · ~700 MB of audio

Writes one row per real audio file, at the real sizes, then removes it again. Read the “quota” and “% of quota used” numbers above.

3 · two tabs at once

In the second tab, tap “force open anyway”. A clear error is the good outcome; anything else is the bad one.

4 · losing everything, and getting it back

“Delete ALL storage” does what an eviction does. Safe: everything here rebuilds from the export on the PC.
